Days to first harvest

Counted from the day the crop goes into its bed or tower. Every number is used as it stands: nothing is added or taken off for the month or the house. Grey is the default; where a tunnel, greenhouse or tower has no number of its own it shows the field figure, so set the ones that differ. A variety with its own figure keeps it; a planting with no variety uses the crop row.
